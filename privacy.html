Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025. WhichPhone is designed to be private by default.

1

No data collection

WhichPhone does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All device detection happens entirely within your browser using JavaScript.

2

No server upload

Your phone model, screen size, CPU cores, RAM, GPU, and OS version are never sent to any server. The detection logic runs locally on your device.

3

No cookies or tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics, fingerprinting scripts, or third-party trackers. There is nothing to opt out of because nothing is tracked.

4

No account required

You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use WhichPhone.

5

Local processing only

All data shown in the results panel is generated on the fly in your browser and discarded when you close or refresh the page.

6

Third-party links

Our site may contain links to external sites (such as email). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.
