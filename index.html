How it works

Four quick steps, all inside your browser.

  1. Open the page

    Visit whichphone.github.io on the phone you want to check.

  2. Tap detect

    Press the button to start the scan.

  3. We read signals

    User agent, client hints, screen and hardware values are collected.

  4. View your specs

    Your device report appears right below the button.

Frequently asked questions

How does WhichPhone detect my phone model?

It reads information your browser already shares, such as the user agent, client hints, screen size and pixel ratio, then matches it against known devices.

Why does my iPhone show several possible models?

Safari does not reveal the exact iPhone model. Several models share the same screen, so we show the matching group.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. Detection runs entirely in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored.

Does it work on Android phones?

Yes. Chrome-based browsers on Android share a model code, which we use to identify your phone.

Is WhichPhone free?

Yes, it is completely free with no sign-up.
