About WhichPhone

A simpler way to know your phone

WhichPhone is a free, browser-based tool that tells you what phone you are holding — no app store, no account, no data collection.

W

Why we built it

Finding your exact phone model should not require digging through settings or installing an app. We wanted a single button that answers the question instantly.

H

How it works

Your browser already knows a lot: the user agent, client hints, screen size, and hardware details. WhichPhone reads these signals and turns them into a clear, human-readable report.

P

Privacy first

Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, nothing is stored, and there is no tracking. Your device details stay on your device.

F

Always free

WhichPhone is completely free and requires no sign-up. We believe basic device information should be easy to access for everyone.

Our approach

Accuracy over guesses

We match browser signals against known device data. When the exact model cannot be determined (as with some iPhones), we show the closest matching group instead of guessing.

Transparency

The spec fields table on our homepage explains exactly what each value means and where it comes from.

No bloat

One page, one button, one clear report. We keep the tool focused on what it does best.
